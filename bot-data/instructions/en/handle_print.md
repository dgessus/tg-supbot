Printer not printing?
1. Check in the control panel whether your printer is displayed in the system
To do this, go to the control panel ---> select *"Hardware and Sound*" (The view from the top right should be in the *"Category"*) position ---> *Devices and Printers* and check if your printer is there. If it is not there, click the *"Add a printer"* button, select your printer and follow the instructions.
If the printer could not be found, check its connection to the network and the Internet. If the connection is there, create a ticket using the button below and attach your AnyDesk code

2. **If the printer is displayed in the system, but does not print:**
In the *"Devices and Printers"* tab, right-click on your printer and go to the *"Printer Settings"* menu and click on the test print button at the bottom of the window that opens. If the printer is not printing, remove it by right-clicking on it, selecting *"Remove device"* and following the instructions in point 1

If none of the above helped, click on the "🎟 Create ticket" button below this message
